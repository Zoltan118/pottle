# security

if you find a way to move money out of a pot other than to its organiser (goal hit) or back to the
people who paid in (deadline missed), or a way to block either of those, please report it privately.

- **report:** https://github.com/Zoltan118/pottle/security/advisories/new
- please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability
- include the network, the pot id or transaction if there is one, and the steps to reproduce

known limits are listed in [`AUDIT.md`](AUDIT.md). pottle has had no third-party audit yet.
